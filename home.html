The race to the iceCream...

The players: player1, player2

The goal: catching the iceCream by pressing on the numbers of your keys area

The course of the game: the players need to press the numbers according the iceCream ascending

Player1, your keys area is in the top of the keyboard

Player2, your keys area is in the right side of the keyboard

The winner: the first who catch 10 iceCreams
